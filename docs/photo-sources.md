# Photo sourcing and use

## Catalog shipped in this revision

42 color photographs: 40 from Pexels and two from AllGo on Unsplash. Ten are additions beyond the earlier private shortlists. Photos are used as credited clothing references, not model endorsements, attractiveness ratings, product listings or a stock-download service.

Source and license links appear on every image. `src/lib/photo-catalog.ts` is the active metadata; `scripts/photo-assets.json` is the matching download manifest. Research-shortlist entries are not automatically approved for the app. General copyright permission is not individual verification of model, property, trademark or other third-party rights. Do not claim that releases were obtained.

## Selection and review

All selected photos were visually reviewed in contact sheets; individual detail checks found further issues. Grayscale photo 31063303 is excluded. Photos 6520931, 18404805, 30677128, 14240788, 17037280, 34608849, 17049703, 13024431 and 18220798 are excluded for visible/possible recognizable marks or graphics requiring more review. This is a conservative editorial hold, not a claim that each photo is unlawful. The 18220798 sunglasses concern was found in independent review and removed before publication.

Photo 5254744 was corrected to a camisole and midi skirt with heeled sandals; its visible heels now obey the No heels filter. Unknown shoes or bottoms fail closed under the related exclusion. Do not infer fiber, exact product, size, age or biological sex from a photo. Women/men labels describe the editorial clothing range; smaller/mid/fuller are approximate visual references, not verified measurements. Body coverage remains incomplete, especially fuller-bodied menswear and full-outfit views.

## Files and publication

Original aspect ratios and colors are retained; only orientation normalization, resizing and WebP compression are applied. No synthetic body reshaping or desaturation. Detail shots are labeled distinctly. Image files and photo-containing screenshots are ignored by Git rather than redistributed as a standalone stock-image collection. Code, source links, credited metadata and test instructions are committed. The local production build serves licensed images as part of this app.

Run `python scripts/fetch-photos.py` after installing Pillow to provision the local assets. Availability of external sources is a dependency for fresh clones; existing production copies are local and do not depend on third-party image requests. No API key or paid subscription is required for these assets. No bulk image export or resale feature is included.

If a contributor reports a rights concern, remove that specific reference from the catalog and manifest and retire its asset; preserve unrelated user work. Before expanding to advertising, sponsored endorsements, model-specific claims, stock redistribution or machine-learning training, perform a separate rights review. Do not treat this app's use as permission for those other uses.
