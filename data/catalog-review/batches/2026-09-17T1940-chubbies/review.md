# Chubbies first bounded intake

Directly inspected 24 candidates on two bound sheets; enlarged original images at ordinals 2 and 4 confirm broader builds while leaving waist and shoulder/hip axes unknown under loose shirts. The first listing contains 250 products; at most 12 previously unseen apparel pages were selected, sampling first/third gallery positions. Every listing decision, download outcome and raw snapshot hash is retained. This is not an exhaustive source search.

Six photos admitted: three masculine casual collared-shirt ensembles and three unclear basics/layers. Five have build labels, including one at 2.5 and one at 3; none supports all three required axes. Flat products, fabric details, backs and repetitive poses are excluded. No prior Chubbies admissions exist. These counts describe photos, not distinct models or gender identities.

The source adds useful fuller-build casual coverage, but first/third galleries also contain many flat products. Next searches should investigate alternate full-front galleries and fitted tops on new product pages, recording every selected/deferred position. Loose silhouettes cannot be converted into body-filter matches. Not deployed.
